Two things called Grok

The app and the bots

Both carry the Grok name, but they're different products. xAI's own help page says so.

The Grok app

Chat, talk out loud with Voice, make pictures and videos with Imagine, and look things up on the web and X. It runs on iPhone, Android and grok.com. This is the one Julie needs.

Grok Bot

Always-on helpers that each have their own computer in the cloud, like Ara and Elon. They do jobs while you sleep, run routines and ping your phone when they're done.

🍎 iPhone

Julie's iPhone, her own loginYou're here

About 3 minutes. Her chats, her history, her settings. Nothing mixed up with Andrew's.

Golden rule: Julie signs in with her own Apple ID, Google account or email. Never Andrew's. Each login keeps its own chats, and any subscription belongs to the login that paid for it.
  1. 1

    Get the app

    Open the App Store, search Grok and pick Grok AI (developer X Corp.). Tap Get. It's free and needs iOS 17 or newer.

    Open Grok in the App Store ↗
  2. 2

    Open it and sign in as Julie

    Choose Sign in with Apple using her own Apple ID. That's the easiest on an iPhone. Google, X or email work too.

    💡 If she pays for SuperGrok through the App Store, she needs to keep using Sign in with Apple, or the app won't find her subscription.
  3. 3

    Say yes to the microphone

    When it asks, allow the microphone so Voice works. She can also allow photos so she can show Grok a picture.

  4. 4

    Try Voice

    Tap the voice button and just talk, like a phone call. Ask something easy first, like "Plan a weekend away near Melbourne for two."

  5. 5

    Show it a photo

    Tap + beside the message box and add a photo, a receipt or a PDF, then ask what she wants to know about it.

  6. 6

    Make a picture

    Open Imagine and describe what she wants. Pictures come out with a small Grok watermark. That's normal and can't be turned off.

Stuck? Close and reopen the app, sign out and back in, or reinstall. That's xAI's own first fix. Sign-in methods are managed at accounts.x.ai.
📱 Android

Andrew's Fold 8 / Flip 6You're here

Same app, same idea. Andrew signs in with his own account.

  1. 1

    Get it from Google Play

    Search Grok in the Play Store, or tap the button below.

    Open Grok in Google Play ↗
  2. 2

    Sign in once

    Use the same login on the phone and on grok.com, so chats and any subscription show up in both places.

  3. 3

    Talk, show, make

    Voice, + for files and photos, and Imagine all work the same way as on the iPhone.

  4. 4

    Bigger jobs: hand them to the bots

    For work that runs on its own (routines, checking things every morning, building pages), message Ara or Elon. That's Grok Bot, not the Grok app.

Usage: Settings → Usage shows how much of the weekly allowance is used across Chat, Voice and Imagine.

Bonus video from Andrew

Use it properly: a thinking partner, not a search box

Jay Shetty's point: most people use AI like Google. Use it to think things through out loud. The video talks about other chat apps, but the same prompts work in Grok, and Voice makes it even quicker.

Life audit

Score each part of life from 1 to 10 and let it find the patterns.

"Here's my 1-to-10 on health, family, work, money and fun. What patterns do you see, and what's the one change that would help most?"

Spot the repeat

Describe a few times you got stuck. It looks for the one pattern behind them.

Your own rules

Pull 5 to 7 principles out of your real wins and regrets, then read them each morning.

Practise the hard chat

Get it to play the other person so you can rehearse a tough conversation first.

Tiny habit buddy

Pick one habit, start it tiny, and have it check in with you and track your streak.

"If I disappear for a few days, say: welcome back, no judgement, let's start again."

Bad-day reset

When a feeling's bigger than the problem, ask it to go one question at a time to find what's really behind it.

Straight from the video: it's a mirror, not a replacement for people or a therapist. It can also hype you up for no reason, so push back when that happens.

Bots that work while you sleep

Airdev's walkthrough of Grok Bot. This is how Ara and Elon work.

Their own computer

Each bot lives on a cloud computer that's always on, so the work keeps going after you close your phone.

Routines

Jobs on a timer, like "every morning at 8, brief me on today's meetings."

Plug-ins

One-tap connections to calendar, email, Drive and more.

Passwords go in the box

Never paste a password into the chat. Use the secure box the bot gives you.

Teach it your way

Give it an example or template once and it keeps it on its computer for next time.

Pings your phone

Push notifications when a job's done or something has changed.
