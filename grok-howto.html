Jobs that run themselves

Automations, skills, connectors

  1. ⏰

    Automations

    Describe a job once and Grok runs it on a schedule (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly) or when a matching email lands. Each run is a full conversation saved to history, and it reports back by email, app notification, both or neither.

    💡 Set one up straight from chat: "Check the news every morning and flag anything about pricing."
    Source: Automations in Grok
  2. 🧰

    Skills

    Teach Grok something once and it remembers in every conversation. Built-in skills make Word docs, slide decks, spreadsheets and PDFs. When it's nailed a format, tell it to save what it learned as a skill.

    Source: Skills in web, iOS and Android
  3. 🔌

    Connectors

    Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Calendar (one Google Workspace connector), plus Outlook, OneDrive, SharePoint, Notion, GitHub and Linear. Or bring your own MCP server. On the phone: Settings → Connectors.

    Source: Connectors in web, iOS and Android

🍎 iPhone

Julie's iPhone, her own loginYou're here

Her own account, her chats, her settings. Nothing shared with Andrew's.

Her own account, her own plan: Julie gets her own Grok account on the free tier, not Andrew's SuperGrok Heavy plan. Subscriptions belong to the login that bought them. If she wants more later, she can choose her own plan (SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy, as listed in the App Store).
  1. 1

    Get the app

    In the App Store, find Grok AI (seller X Corp.). It's free and needs iOS 17 or later.

    Grok AI in the App Store ↗
  2. 2

    Sign in as Julie

    Use Sign in with Apple on her own Apple ID, or her own email, Google or X account. Never Andrew's.

    💡 If she ever pays through the App Store, she has to keep using Sign in with Apple, or the app won't find her subscription.
  3. 3

    Allow the microphone

    So voice conversations work. Allow photos too, so she can show Grok a picture.

  4. 4

    Voice, photos, Imagine

    Talk to it, tap + to add a photo or PDF, or describe a picture to make. Generated images carry a Grok watermark, which can't be removed.

  5. 5

    Plug in her apps (optional)

    Settings → Connectors links her own Gmail, Calendar or Drive.

  6. 6

    Same chats on the web

    Signing in to grok.com with the same login shows the same history, because it syncs across web, iOS and Android.

Stuck? xAI's first fix: restart the app, sign out and back in, or reinstall. Sign-in methods live at accounts.x.ai.
