What Grok actually is

Grok is the AI assistant made by xAI (Elon Musk’s company).https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex

Its job is to help you understand things, get answers, and get work done.UNSOURCED

You can use it on the website (grok.com), the phone apps (iOS and Android), and inside X.https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5-everywherehttps://x.ai/grok

Sign in once and everything stays the same across all of them.https://x.ai/grok

xAI’s mission is simple: build AI to understand the universe and speed up human scientific discovery.https://x.ai/company

Grok is the tool they give people so they can ask almost anything and get useful answers without the usual corporate filters.UNSOURCED

How Grok was built (the short version)

• xAI started in 2023 with the mission “understand the universe”.https://x.ai/company

• They trained early models (Grok-0, then Grok-1) on big computer clusters.https://x.ai/news/grok

• Later versions got better at reasoning, coding, and using tools (searching the web and X in real time).https://x.ai/grok

• They built huge GPU clusters (like Colossus in Memphis) so the models could get smarter.UNSOURCED

• Today the main model is around Grok 4.7. It can chat, search live, think harder when needed, make images and short videos, talk with voice, read files you upload, and connect to your other tools.https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7https://x.ai/companyhttps://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3https://x.ai/news/grok-connectors

• Grok is designed to be more direct and less filtered than most other AIs. It prefers truth-seeking over being polite or safe for the sake of it.UNSOURCED

What you can actually do with it

• Chat about anything — questions, ideas, writing, problems.UNSOURCED

• Ask it to search the live web and X and cite sources.https://x.ai/grok

• Upload PDFs, images, spreadsheets, code, audio and get summaries or answers from them.https://x.ai/grok

• Make images and short videos with Grok Imagine.https://x.ai/grok

• Talk to it with voice (hands-free).https://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3

• Connect your email, calendar, Drive, etc. so it can work with your real stuff.https://x.ai/news/grok-connectors

• Use Grok Bot (on higher plans) — always-on AI teammates that can do work while you’re away.https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricinghttps://x.ai/news/grok-bot-more-plans

• Coding help and tools.UNSOURCED

Subscriptions (what you pay for)

Grok is free to start with limits.https://x.ai/pricing

Paid plans give you more usage and extra features.https://x.ai/pricing

Plans and prices change, so always check grok.com for the latest.UNSOURCED

Common tiers you will see:

The current list is Free $0, Plus $8/mo, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200. Plus does not include Grok Bot.https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing

• Free — basic chat and some features, limited.https://x.ai/pricing

New customers now pick The X Subscription. The $30/mo tier is Premium ($30/mo or $300/yr), which gives "the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro, and X Premium+". SuperGrok only carries on for people who already have it, and no official page shows its standalone price.https://x.ai/pricinghttps://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricinghttps://x.ai/news/x-subscription

The higher tiers are now Super $100/mo (15x Plus usage, priority at peak times) and Ultra $200/mo (50x Plus usage). SuperGrok Heavy only appears as an existing plan and as a business licence type.https://x.ai/pricinghttps://docs.x.ai/grok/management

• There is also The X Subscription that bundles Grok + Cursor coding + X Premium benefits.https://x.ai/news/x-subscription

You pay for more capacity and faster/better access.https://x.ai/pricing

How to use Grok properly (simple habits)

1. Sign in once on grok.com or the app. Everything syncs.https://x.ai/grok

2. Be clear and specific. Short, direct questions work best.UNSOURCED

3. If you want current info, just ask. It will search live when it needs to.https://x.ai/grok

4. Upload files when you have them instead of pasting huge text.https://x.ai/grok

5. For hard problems, tell it to think step by step or use deeper reasoning.UNSOURCED

6. Check the sources it cites when it matters.https://x.ai/grok

7. Use voice when your hands are busy.https://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3

8. Connect tools (email, calendar, Drive) so it can act on your real data.https://x.ai/news/grok-connectors

9. On higher plans, hand repeated work to a Grok Bot so it keeps going while you’re away.https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricinghttps://x.ai/news/grok-bot-more-plans

10. Watch your weekly usage. Higher plans give more room. Don’t burn it all on one long rebuild.https://x.ai/pricinghttps://docs.x.ai/grok/faq

Why Grok is strong (the real reasons)

• Real-time access to the web and X — it can see what’s happening now, not just old training data.https://x.ai/grok

• Less filtered answers. It is built to pursue truth rather than avoid anything controversial.UNSOURCED

• Strong at reasoning, coding, and using tools (search, code execution, etc.).UNSOURCED

• One account works everywhere (web, phone, X).https://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-4-5-everywhere

• Image, video, voice, and file understanding in the same place.https://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3

• Grok Bot for ongoing work.https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-more-plans

• Built by the same company that runs X and has massive compute (Colossus).https://x.ai/colossushttps://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex

Quick start if you’re 15

1. Go to grok.com or download the app.https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5-everywherehttps://x.ai/grok

2. Sign in (X account or email).https://x.ai/grok

3. Ask something real: “Explain black holes simply” or “What’s happening with [topic] right now?”UNSOURCED

4. Try uploading a photo or PDF and asking about it.https://x.ai/grok

5. Turn on voice and talk.https://x.ai/grokhttps://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3

6. If you use it a lot, look at SuperGrok for higher limits and Grok Bot.UNSOURCED
